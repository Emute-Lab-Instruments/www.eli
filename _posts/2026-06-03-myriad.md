We're very happy to announce that [Myriad](/myriad) is launching today. Myriad is a 'pataphonic' multioscillator -a stereo sound source with an experimental twist that moves from standard waveforms to monster drones and glassy moving textures. It represents our research into new sound synthesis methods with 'bitwave' synthesis, and application of dynamic artificial life algorithms as meta-modulation systems.

It's available now in built form from shops (distributed by Signal Sounds), or in kit form from Thonk.
